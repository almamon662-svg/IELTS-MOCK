The Importance of Sleep

Sleep is an essential part of human life. Scientists have found that getting enough sleep can improve memory, concentration and overall health. Most adults need between seven and nine hours of sleep every night.


In modern society, however, many people do not get enough sleep. Long working hours, excessive use of mobile phones and stress are some of the common reasons for sleep deprivation.


Lack of sleep can affect both physical and mental performance. People who regularly sleep too little may have difficulty concentrating and may also become more vulnerable to illness.


Researchers therefore recommend maintaining a regular sleeping schedule. Avoiding electronic devices before bedtime and creating a quiet sleeping environment can also help people sleep better.
